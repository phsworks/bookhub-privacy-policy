BookHub Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2025 — PHS Development

BookHub is a Chrome extension that helps you organize and understand your bookmarks using AI. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

What we collect

  • Email address and password hash — when you create an account, used to identify you and manage your subscription.
  • Bookmark URLs and titles — sent to our API to categorize and describe your bookmarks using AI. We do not store your bookmarks on our servers; they are stored locally in your browser.
  • Usage count — we track how many bookmarks you have analyzed to enforce the free plan limit.
  • Subscription status — whether you are on the free or pro plan, managed via Stripe.

What we do not collect

  • We do not collect your browsing history.
  • We do not sell your data to third parties.
  • We do not track you across websites.
  • We do not store the content of web pages you visit.

How we use your data

  • AI categorization — bookmark URLs and titles are sent to our API, which uses the Anthropic Claude API to generate a category and short description. This data is not stored on our servers after processing.
  • Account management — your email is used to identify your account and send subscription-related emails via Stripe.
  • Payments — payment processing is handled by Stripe. We do not store credit card information. See Stripe's privacy policy.

Data storage

Your bookmarks are stored locally in your browser using Chrome's local storage API. They are not uploaded to our servers. Your account data (email, plan status, usage count) is stored in a secure database hosted by Neon.

Data retention

Your account data is retained as long as your account exists. You can delete your account at any time from the BookHub settings, which permanently removes all your data from our servers.

Third-party services

  • Anthropic — AI processing of bookmark URLs and titles. See Anthropic's privacy policy.
  • Stripe — payment processing. See Stripe's privacy policy.
  • Neon — database hosting. See Neon's privacy policy.

Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data at any time. To delete your account and all associated data, go to BookHub Settings and click "Delete account". For any other requests, contact us at the address below.
